Questions

Good questions.

Is this right for a small breeder?

That’s exactly who it’s built for: breeders raising roughly one to four litters a year, of any breed. Start with your first litter and add the details as you go.

How long does setup take?

You can add your first litter and a few buyers in a few minutes, then fill in the rest as you go. There’s nothing to install and no data to migrate.

What happens after the free trial?

Your trial ends without a charge. Choose Starter at $39/month or Pro at $89/month to continue. Payment details are only needed when you choose to subscribe, and your records stay in your account.

Do my buyers need an account to sign?

No. They get a link, review the agreement, and sign online from any device. They don’t create an account.

Do I need Pro to create contracts?

No. Both plans let you write or paste your own sales agreement and send it for online signing. Pro also includes AI drafting. Always review your agreement before sending.

How do I track buyer deposits?

Collect deposits directly from your buyers using your usual payment method. Once you receive one, mark it as received on the litter waitlist. LitterDesk doesn’t handle the payment or take a cut.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. LitterDesk runs in your phone’s browser, so you can check a waitlist or update a litter wherever you are.

Who can see my buyers’ information?

Only you. Your records are private to your account and we never sell your data. Details are in our privacy policy.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month. Manage your subscription from the Payments page in your account.
