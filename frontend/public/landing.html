A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.

Is this right for a small breeder?

That’s exactly who it’s built for: breeders raising roughly one to four litters a year, of any breed. Start with your first litter and add the details as you go.

What happens after the free trial?

Your trial ends without a charge. Choose Starter at $39/month or Pro at $89/month to continue. Payment details are only needed when you choose to subscribe. Your records stay in your account.

Do I need Pro to create contracts?

No. Both plans let you write or paste your own sales agreement and send it for online signing. Pro also includes AI drafting. Always review your agreement before sending.

How do I track buyer deposits?

Collect deposits directly from your buyers using your usual payment method. Once you receive a deposit, mark it as received on the litter waitlist. Both plans include deposit tracking.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month. Manage your subscription through the billing controls on your Payments page.
